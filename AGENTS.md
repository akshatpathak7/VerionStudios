# Verion Studios website guidelines

- Use white backgrounds, black highlights, and neutral gray supporting text. The supplied red logo is the only color accent.
- Use soft rounded rectangles: 24px panel corners and 14px button corners. Do not use pill-shaped buttons.
- Write direct, specific, professional copy in complete natural sentences. No em dashes, vague hero claims, fragmented slogans, or “not just X” / “we don’t just X” framing.
- Never invent reviews, customer activity, counters, metrics, clients, or endorsements.
- Do not add gradients, emoji icons, cursor animations, AI images, or made-with-AI badges.
- Keep motion limited to short hover/focus transitions. Respect reduced motion and maintain normal scrolling.
- Keep privacy and terms limited to verified website behavior and inquiries; client service terms belong in separate agreements.
- Preserve the existing contact emails, supplied logo, and video editing as the primary service.
- Validate with `npx next build` for Vercel. Do not push or deploy unless requested.
